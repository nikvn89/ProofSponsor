How ProofSponsor Pays a Creator After a GenLayer Verdict

Sponsored content usually ends with an awkward step: the creator has published the work, and now has to trust the sponsor to pay. ProofSponsor removes that step by keeping the reward inside a GenLayer Intelligent Contract.

The sponsor creates a campaign with a written brief and a fixed reward per approved delivery, then deposits GEN into the campaign pool. The creator publishes a public page, includes a wallet-specific proof marker, and submits the page URL.

GenLayer validators read the page and decide whether it meaningfully satisfies the brief. When they approve it, the contract immediately reserves the reward from the sponsor's pool for that creator. The sponsor can no longer take that amount back.

The creator then withdraws the reserved GEN directly from the contract. If the creator never claims it, the reservation expires after thirty days and returns to the pool. The sponsor can reclaim only what is left unreserved, and only after closing the campaign and after every submitted delivery has a verdict.

Closing a campaign stops new submissions, but it does not stop a delivery that was already submitted from being judged and paid. The verdict, the reservation and the payment are all recorded onchain.
